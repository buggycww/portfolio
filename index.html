02 / Technical lab

Measure the claim.

A pooled allocator started as a simple question: how much of a linked list’s cost is actually the allocator? I built two versions, ran ten trials of 100,000 operations, and split insertion from deletion so the traversal cost did not hide the answer.

Insertion got 9.88× faster. Deletion got 2.0× faster — and the remaining gap was a useful lesson in cache locality, not just allocation.

03 / About

A programmer who cares about the player’s hands.

I’m Renee, a gameplay programmer based in Malaysia. I graduated from Xiamen University Malaysia with a BEng (Hons) in Digital Media Technology, ranked 1st in a cohort of 59, and spent six months as a game programming intern at Virtuos Kuala Lumpur.

My favourite problems sit where systems meet feel: a grid that snaps cleanly, a quadtree that keeps collision honest, a boss phase that telegraphs just enough. I like code that is simple to reason about, cheap to run, and easy for designers to tune.

I’m currently looking for gameplay programmer roles in Malaysia, Singapore, or China — ideally on a C++/Unreal team.
