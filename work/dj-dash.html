DJ Dash!

Role
Solo
Engine
Unity
Platform
Android
Year
2024
Course
Game Design & Development

A 3D endless runner for Android. Michelle the DJ runs through a neon city collecting music notes on her way to the top.

The track builds itself as you run: procedural platforms, randomised obstacles, and a soundtrack that reacts to what you pick up. The further you get, the less time you have to think.

Built in Unity for a game design and development course, and the first game I shipped to a phone.

Features

  • Procedural platform generation
  • Audio visualisation tied to the soundtrack
  • Collectible music notes and special music modes
  • Escalating speed and obstacle patterns
  • High-score chasing
  • Touch controls on Android
