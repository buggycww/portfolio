EcoWorld

Role
Solo design & programming
Engine
Unity
Platform
Meta Quest 3
Year
2026
Award
National 2nd Runner-Up

A mixed-reality ecology game you play on a table. Build a rainforest, change the weather, and watch the ecosystem answer back.

EcoWorld runs on Meta Quest 3 and turns a tabletop into a Southeast Asian rainforest. You place organisms, change the conditions they live in, and watch the food web react. Nothing is scripted: every creature reads its own needs and the current weather, then decides what to do next.

It was my final-year project. The point was cause and effect you can see: change one variable, then follow it through the population, the weather, and the next disaster.

Second Runner-Up at Malaysia Creative Tech Competition 2026, selected from 87 entries across 23 institutions.

Features

  • Organism AI — a state machine per creature, driven by hunger, safety, and the environment
  • Weather and time of day — conditions that change what every organism wants
  • Disaster events — droughts, storms, and fires that stress the ecosystem and force a response
  • In-game reference tools — the ecology information a player needs, without leaving the table
  • Tabletop mixed reality — the simulation sits on a real surface, at real-world scale
  • Playtest-driven UI — what the game shows came from watching players miss it
