Gameplay systems

A souls-like action RPG about a village boy who leaves home to hunt monsters, and finds out who he was really working for.

The combat is built for weight. Attacks chain into strings, shields decide whether you get a turn, and the final boss asks you to use everything you learned. Between fights, an EXP and skill system decides how strong you walk in.

The world carries a layered quest and dialogue system that keeps turning as the story does. What starts as a familiar hero's journey gets much darker by the end.

My part: chainable light and heavy attacks, shield behaviour, and the final boss encounter.

Features

  • Chainable player attacks — light and heavy strings that flow into each other
  • Shield behaviour — blocking, and the cost of getting it wrong
  • Final boss encounter — designed, built, and tuned
  • EXP and skill progression
  • Layered quest and dialogue system
  • Dark, interconnected world built in Unreal Engine
