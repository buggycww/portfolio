A 2D cooking game set in a Malaysian mamak stall. Roti canai, teh tarik, and a queue that never ends.

Every customer walks in with their own order and their own preferences. You take it, cook it, and serve it before they lose patience, while the next table fills up behind them.

Made with a team of four for Merdeka Jam 2026. I worked on systems and gameplay.

Features

  • Order taking, with per-customer preferences
  • Roti canai cooking — flip, fold, serve
  • Drink customisation
  • Customers from different backgrounds, each with their own patience
  • A stall that has to keep running while the queue grows
  • Playable in the browser
