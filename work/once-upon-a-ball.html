Once Upon a Ball

Role
Team project
Engine
Unreal Engine
Genre
Adventure
Year
2026
Chapter
Sleeping Beauty

An adventure game where you roll a prince through reimagined fairytales. The first chapter is Sleeping Beauty.

The kingdom is cursed. You roll through thorn forests, escape a dungeon, and find the path into the castle, because in this world the level design is the story. Every obstacle is part of the world rather than a puzzle box dropped on top of it.

It ends the way a fairytale should: with a boss fight against Maleficent, and a curse to break.

Features

  • Rolling movement built around momentum and physics
  • Environmental puzzles folded into the level itself
  • Thorn forests, dungeons, and castle interiors
  • Story cutscenes between chapters
  • Boss fight against Maleficent
  • Built in Unreal Engine
