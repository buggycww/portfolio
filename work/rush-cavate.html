Genshin Impact: Miliastra Wonderland
Disclosure
NDA

A 3D Bomberman mode inside Genshin Impact's Miliastra Wonderland. Blast the board open, find the hidden objectives, and beat everyone else to them.

The bombs are elemental. Fire beats ice, ice beats water, water beats fire, and matching elements on the board sets off chain reactions. Reading the board is the skill; the explosion is just the payoff.

I worked on it for five months at Virtuos Kuala Lumpur: elemental bomb behaviour, grid placement, range indicators, upgrade paths, skills, and the tutorial.

Features

  • Elemental bombs — Fire, Ice, and Water, with a rock-paper-scissors cycle
  • Chain reactions — match elements to clear a whole board
  • Grid placement, movement, and range indicators
  • Upgrade paths that change how the core bomb loop plays
  • Skills, and a tutorial that teaches the loop the way players meet it
  • Live in-game on the Asia server — code 21621157088
