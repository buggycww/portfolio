Veils of Forgotten Power

Role
Solo
Engine
Unity
Genre
Open-world action-adventure
Year
2024
Levels
Three

An open-world action-adventure starring Pumpkin and Broccoli. Three levels, four systems, and a chase scene you will remember.

Each level stacks puzzles on top of a quest chain, a dialogue system with real choices, and an inventory that gates what you can do next. The last level ends in a chase, then a boss with a branched ending.

Features

  • Inventory system
  • Quest system with multiple objectives
  • Dialogue system with choices
  • Puzzle-driven level design across three levels
  • A chase sequence and a multi-phase boss
  • Branched ending
