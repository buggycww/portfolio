github.com/buggycww/RPG-Warrior

A third-person action RPG in Unreal Engine 5, built on the Gameplay Ability System. Abilities, attributes, and damage are all data.

Every attack is a gameplay ability. Every stat is an attribute. Every damage number is an execution calculation that reads both combatants, then scales by where you are in the combo. Enemies run through the same pipeline as the player.

It is also the project that taught me the cost of ordering: when a montage fires, when a notify turns weapon collision on, and when the effect lands decide whether a hit feels responsive or floaty.

Features

  • Light and heavy axe attacks, chainable into combo strings
  • Attribute set — health, rage, attack power, defense power, damage taken
  • Damage execution calculation with per-combo scaling
  • Equip and unequip abilities, weapon spawning, collision toggled by animation notifies
  • Enemy AI — behaviour tree, blackboard, custom tasks and services
  • Hit pause, camera shake, and hit reactions
  • Data assets and linked animation layers
